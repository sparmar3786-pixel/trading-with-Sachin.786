❓ How to use (Hinglish)

Step 1 — Input: Ek time ka chain bharo aur "Save snapshot". Doosre time ka bharo aur phir save. Ab tumhare paas A (purana) aur B (naya) hai.

Step 2 — Analyze: A aur B choose karo, side (PE BUY / CE BUY) choose karo, "Run rule engine". Engine strikes classify karega, probable seller pressure zone nikalega, opposite-side override check karega, aur ek transparent confidence dega — saare inputs ke saath.

Zaroori: Engine kabhi "exact seller SL" claim nahi karta — sirf probable zone + confidence + evidence. Intraday bars 0 ya data stale ho to confidence hard-cap ho jaata hai aur STALE/NO-DATA badge dikhta hai.

Charges: P/L tab me gross, net aur true breakeven dikhta hai. Defaults approximate — broker ke hisaab se Settings me theek karo.

Journal: Signal ke baad outcome (win/loss/expired) tag karo. Har rule ka hit-rate track hota hai, taaki thresholds calibrate ho sakein.

Methodology (short)

Premium ↓ + OI ↑ = short buildup · Premium ↑ + OI ↓ = short covering · Premium ↑ + OI ↑ = long buildup · Premium ↓ + OI ↓ = long unwinding.

Probable seller pressure zone: jahan premium girte waqt OI concentrate hui, phir premium up + OI down + volume expand hua. Wahi short-covering trigger.

Opposite-side override: PE BUY ke liye, agar nearby CE strikes me premium up + OI down + volume expand ho aur spot key CE strike ke upar chala jaaye → PE signal BLOCK/EXIT/REVERSAL-WATCH.

Seller ke hidden stop-loss ko koi bhi tool exactly nahi jaan sakta — sirf probable zones. Apna khud ka judgement aur risk management use karein.
